RGB video and calibrated camera poses from the TUM RGB-D SLAM Benchmark. J. Sturm, N. Engelhard, F. Endres, W. Burgard and D. Cremers, IROS 2012. Dataset and license: https://cvg.cit.tum.de/data/datasets/rgbd-dataset and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The task authors excerpted native RGB frames, arranged keyframes in a sheet, and expressed poses relative to the first keyframe.

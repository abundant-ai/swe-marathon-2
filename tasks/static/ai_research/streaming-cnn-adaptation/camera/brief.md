The classifier moves between two camera feeds. One uses a serviced capture board and the other still supplies ordinary RGB. The serviced board has a fixed response. An operator can switch feeds during a deployment without notifying the updater. Before the service the CNN was trained on ordinary RGB. The board can change channel routing, color mixing and relative channel gains. The upstream video here is real CORe50 footage. The board response is simulated.

When supplied, preview.png is the service technician's paired calibration sheet. Each field in the upper half shows the original capture. The field at the same position in the lower half shows the same exposure after the board conversion. The preview contains different object captures from the deployment stream. Inspect it during authoring and use the evidence to decide how to adapt the CNN as frames arrive. The deployed updater has no preview file. Retain performance on original RGB captures as well as on the serviced board. During development you can simulate a board on source or development video and compare your updater with frozen inference.

Train the CORe50 CNN in /app/work and commission a portable stock scanner for two rooms. Headquarters uses ten household-object categories. Each room gives those categories its own stock IDs. Both rooms share the same scanner and interchangeable camera modules. An inventory frame can be byte-for-byte identical in the two rooms and require a different stock ID.

**Read the photographic handover.** Open inventory.png, preview.png and site-handovers.png with an image viewer when available. The two columns of inventory.png show two object views per local stock ID, in order from 0 to 9. The left and right columns belong to rooms 1 and 2. The halves of preview.png show two example camera responses before and after service, photographed in different orders. The two square cards in site-handovers.png correspond to the inventory columns. They contain different arrangements of the same printed colors.

**Train and deliver the classifier.** Write modular Python for source training, handover interpretation and the online weight update. Run train.py on source.npz and save trained.npz using core_model.save. Source x is native 32 × 32 RGB, y gives the headquarters category, and ids gives the session, object and frame. Source and development capture information is in public-splits.json. The clean trainer receives only train.py, core_model.py and source.npz and has two CPU cores for 900 seconds. The grader tests the delivered and retrained models against two independently trained headquarters CNNs. Both of your models must come within five accuracy points of the weaker reference. A clean retraining must agree on at least 80% of the delivered model's headquarters predictions and come within five points of its accuracy. Your training program must learn the category assignments in the provided data even when the camera appearance and local vocabulary change.

**Run online.** Implement Updater(model) and update(self, image) in online.py. Every observation is one CPU float32 RGB tensor of shape (1,3,32,32) in [0,1]. The CNN predicts each stock frame before the updater receives it. The first observation is a card-replacement photograph. It shows two columns, each pairing one archived room card (top) with its newly printed replacement (bottom). The columns may change order. Each square has six rows and six columns of swatches. In the 32-pixel photograph, the four 12-pixel squares start at horizontal and vertical offsets 2 and 18. Match the archived cards to the photographic handover and retain their new counterparts. A second commissioning of the same scanner can assign those new cards differently. At a handover the operator photographs the current room's reference card under the active scanner response. Chart exposures carry no inventory target. They arrive through the same image interface as stock frames. A photographed card may be rotated, off-center, noisy or partly covered. Scanner modules and exposure can change during a room visit. Once the card leaves view, inventory frames contain no room ID. Retain the selected room's stock assignment and maintain the camera correction until the next visual handover. The service photographs are simulated and the inventory video consists of real CORe50 observations.

At deployment you receive the fixed CNN and a stream of pixels. Compile measurements from the public handover into online.py during authoring. The RGB adapter and stock-logit transformation in core_model.py can be edited with the other learned CNN parameters and normalization buffers. Each submitted Python source and the checkpoint must fit in 512 KiB. The updater has 35 seconds to initialize, 145 seconds in total and 12 seconds per update for up to 247 inventory and chart observations. The worker runs offline.

**Verify both itineraries.** The operator visits one room, moves to the other and returns. Either room can come first. Object feeds run as clips or interleave chronologically. Improve class-balanced accuracy in every itinerary and presentation schedule over the frozen CNN, including first appearances, fresh captures at each visit and continuing to update after each relocation. After each relocation, also improve on the previous visit’s CNN held fixed for the entire new visit. Changes in learned weights must improve predictions over a model receiving only the normalization-buffer updates, including on your submitted checkpoint. Keep headquarters recognition immediately after a handover and at each visit's end. Ten points of improvement in each required comparison earns full credit after deducting the largest headquarters loss beyond two points. The submitted CNN is compared with the mean frozen accuracy of two independent starts. For inventory frames whose room IDs differ, recover both correct IDs on at least 35% of paired presentations in each schedule, averaging recalls by stock ID in each room. Recover both correct IDs at the same rate when only the initial replacement photograph changes and all later stream pixels are identical. The weakest result across checkpoints and conditions determines acceptance.

Use python practice.py --schedule clips or --schedule switches to rehearse on labeled public footage and the practice cards. The practice stock IDs and cards are separate from the production handover.

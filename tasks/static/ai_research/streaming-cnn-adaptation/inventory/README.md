# Stock-ID commissioning on streaming CORe50

The camera-only predecessor allowed GPT-6.2-sol xhigh to score 1.0 using video highlights and hand color in the absence of the preview. This pilot introduces a separate piece of information the video cannot identify. Two simulated inventory stations use their own stock IDs for the same ten physical categories. The authoring photographs identify the local assignments. Two installations replay the same bytes in the same order with different assignments. This models a recognition service that must change its output vocabulary while retaining headquarters recognition. It is a research pilot on small handheld-object images. [Predecessor and optical controls](https://www.oddish.app/experiments/a930a49a)

## Data and verification

The CORe50 mini archive is identified by MD5 d89d34cdc0281fa84074430e9a22b728. Six original sessions supply labeled training, with entire per-session object captures reserved for the source probe. Sessions 9 and 11 supply development clips. The first and returning station uses different objects in session 3. The middle segment uses session 10. Each photographic stock card contains two real exposures from another object capture, without embedded category names. Board responses and stock assignments are simulated. The cameras are identical in the two installations. The scored target installation is fixed across the correct, swapped and absent photographic conditions. The swapped stock manifest depicts the alternate installation with an identical calibration sheet. The absent condition omits both sheets. [Dataset and attribution](https://vlomonaco.github.io/core50/)

The two fixed source CNNs use the public trainer with seeds 37 and 73. The added RGB and output residuals initialize to zero, preserving the source predictions. Training is replayed from the submitted script and public source captures. The required source competence floor is the weaker fixed source accuracy minus five percentage points. A separate fresh run with renumbered training labels must come within fifteen points of that floor on correspondingly relabeled source probes. This establishes functional dependence on the supplied labels for the tested intervention. It cannot recover the internal training history of an arbitrary program.

For each checkpoint the verifier computes class-balanced accuracy on complete frame schedules, first appearances and independent paused object probes. A fixed-state control starts each later segment with the CNN that was active just before the switch. Its predictions on that entire segment measure the contribution from continued updating. The official result is the weakest required improvement on the weakest eligible start, divided by ten percentage points and clipped to [0,1]. The largest headquarters accuracy loss above two points is subtracted before scaling. Corrupt trusted fixtures and trusted-worker startup errors propagate as infrastructure failures. A failed candidate program can earn zero. The report keeps the source, training, deployment and alternate-installation evidence separate.

## Hackability assessment

High priority. An updater can infer the camera channels from the natural images. It still has no observation revealing a station's administrative stock IDs. A photo-free program could guess a particular manifest. The two-installation experiment and the separate balanced ten-manifest probe quantify different claims. A single blind failure cannot establish a universal bound for this fixed deployment.

High priority. A strong source classifier, buffer updates, or an edit made only on startup can raise absolute accuracy. Two independent source checkpoints, buffer-only forwards, and the pre-switch frozen controls test those explanations. Staged training and deployed code run under separate disposable identities. Trusted code computes predictions from bounded numerical state, owns the labels and writes the scores. Exact agreement of two rebuilt sources alone cannot establish label use, which motivated the renumbering intervention.

Medium priority. Public practice uses separate captures and simulated boards. Its stock mapping is supplied by the researcher for their own rehearsal. The development outputs cannot reveal the production manifest. The CORe50 source corpus is public, so prior knowledge of objects or an effective pretrained visual recognition method is possible. Correct decoding and lasting improvement are the intended behaviors.

## Development record

November 27, 2026. A public construction check replayed 240 identical observations under ten stock assignments. A fixed blind prediction achieved exactly 10% across all assignments, an image decoder reached 37.08%, and known assignments reached 55%. These are public feasibility measurements with a frozen CNN. Novelty against all adjacent benchmarks and the repository's five-day workload objective remain unverified.

The first matched release received an implementation-stage Harbor assessment. Novelty was unassessed. Verifiability, realism, difficulty and initial feasibility each scored 3/5. Confidence was high for realism and medium for the other three assessed dimensions. The revised first-appearance and arrival-probe measurements make those ratings historical. ## Matched-image screen with order-specific acceptance

On the third release the reference scored 1.0 with the correct photographs and 0.0 with swapped or absent photographs. All three no-ops scored zero. GPT-6.2-sol xhigh scored 1.0 on its first trial. The submitted CNN reached 57.4–63.9% stock accuracy over the four combinations of schedule and station order. Its own frozen CNN scored 11.2–13.2%. Clean retraining reproduced every tensor and every held-out headquarters prediction. [Experiment](https://www.oddish.app/experiments/2886acf8) · [Reference and controls](golden-reference.json) · [Model measurements](v3-model-screen.json)

The submitted updater compiled a camera inverse and a ten-category stock permutation for each station. It installed those weights on a camera switch and returned immediately on later frames from the same station. That meets the category-level task contract. It also explains why the task could give full credit without further learning within a station. The physical-item successor places two members of each category on different stock cards and compares ongoing adaptation with a photo-conditioned frozen matcher.

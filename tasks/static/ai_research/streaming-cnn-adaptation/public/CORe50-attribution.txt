Original paper: CORe50: a New Dataset and Benchmark for Continuous Object Recognition (CoRL 2017), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v78/lomonaco17a.html

This pilot uses the CORe50 32 × 32 RGB derivative with selected recording sessions,
category-level labels, constructed clip orders, and resized preview mosaics.
The service-camera variants apply simulated channel response matrices to real
RGB frames. These are benchmark modifications by the task authors.

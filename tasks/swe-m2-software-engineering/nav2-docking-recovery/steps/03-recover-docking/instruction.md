# Recover from an interrupted docking attempt

A pallet can block the rear approach, the odometry source can become unavailable or restart, and a charger can make contact without conducting. Keep the preceding behaviors and add guarded motion and retries.

The observation can now include `odom: null` and a `clearance` object with `front_m` and `rear_m`, the current free distance from the robot boundary along its travel direction. When `odom` is null, send zero velocity. If its epoch changes, invalidate the old track and wait for a fresh detection in the new frame before resuming the approach. Do not use pose differences across epochs. A temporary interruption does not end the mission while time remains.

If the configuration has `recovery`, fields are `guard_m`, `max_attempts`, `retreat_m` and `retreat_speed_mps`. `max_attempts` includes the original approach. For each move, the corresponding directional clearance must be at least `guard_m`. An unavailable clearance or a blocked direction stops motion in that direction. Honor the guard while moving forward as well as while backing up. A robot in switch contact remains stationary unless performing the forward recovery retreat.

When a configured charge attempt times out, and the mission has remaining attempts and time, drive forward away from the dock. Use valid odometry to measure the distance covered by the retreat. Request no rotation and no speed above `retreat_speed_mps` on this retreat. Pause when the front guard is unavailable, blocked, or odometry is unavailable. If the odometry epoch changes mid-retreat, restart measuring at the first valid pose in the new frame. Before retrying, complete at least `retreat_m` of measured retreat and require fresh dock acquisition from the new staging position. Once attempts have been used or the deadline has passed, report `failed` and stop. A blocked approach without contact may wait for clearance until the mission deadline without consuming a retry.

The three example files use cylinder-center geometry from World 0 of the BARN
Navigation Challenge repository:
https://github.com/Daffan/the-barn-challenge/blob/main/jackal_helper/worlds/BARN/world_0.world

The world, Jackal models and Gazebo plugins are not bundled.

- barn-world-0-crop.json rasterizes a crop of the world and gives it
  task-specific bounds, robot dimensions and endpoints.
- field-world-0.json and movers-world-0.json keep the cylinder centers, moved
  into the task's arena with the start-area wall removed. They add a task
  sensor model, motion limits, endpoints and a deadline. The moving obstacles
  in movers-world-0.json are not part of BARN.

# Transfer control of a moving two-drive lift under load

Extend `/app/control_recovery` with a native Jazzy controller chain for a loaded vertical lift. The two drives support opposite ends of a compliant beam. When the right absolute-feedback branch restarts, a conventional fallback can hold both drives safely and still miss the transfer. Continue tracking the lift trajectory from the remaining feedback. Restore the normal chain after verifying fresh feedback from the replacement branch. Keep the lower drive controller active through both transfers and avoid a jump in its actuator forces.

Preserve the earlier commands and native package. Put the new source under `/app/control_recovery/native/coupled_handover`. Separate the plant feedback and authority handling, degraded estimation, force control, and native mode changes in focused code. The paired hardware is simulated and is supplied by the caller. The manager and its interface claims use ROS 2 Jazzy.

## Native components and topics

Build an ament CMake package named `coupled_handover`. Export `coupled_handover/ForcePair` as a `controller_interface::ChainableControllerInterface`, loaded as `pair`. It claims `left/effort` and `right/effort` and exports `pair/left_effort` and `pair/right_effort` as native command references. The paired controller is one persistent lower layer. Hold the two last valid forces during a brief reference gap, preserve the force state when the upper owner changes, and limit the physical output. Initialize the force state from the supplied static loads.

Export `coupled_handover/LiftSupervisor` as a `controller_interface::ControllerInterface`. Two instances named `tracking` and `bridge` set ROS parameter `policy` to the corresponding name. Each claim both reference interfaces from the lower controller. Only one upper instance may own them. The ROS parameters `coupling_stiffness_n_per_m` and `coupling_damping_ns_per_m` are the calibrated positive stiffness and damping of the beam. Use the following state interfaces:

- `left/position`, `right/position` in metres and `left/velocity`, `right/velocity` in metres per second. The right sample may appear current during a restart even while its epoch is retired. Left feedback remains valid.
- `feedback/strain` is the signed internal force in newtons. It is positive when the left-end deflection is greater by enough to stretch the beam. Its value follows `k * (q_left - q_right) + c * (v_left - v_right)`. It remains available through a right feedback restart.
- `feedback/goal` and `feedback/goal_velocity` are the lift height reference and reference speed. Height is the mean of the two end positions. `feedback/weight_left` and `feedback/weight_right` are calibrated static load estimates in newtons.
- `feedback/epoch`, `feedback/required_epoch`, and `feedback/healthy` describe the reported right sensor epoch, the independent device authority floor, and observed sample health. `feedback/sample` advances on transport updates. Normal tracking may use right position and velocity only when the epoch equals the positive authority floor and health is 1. It must stop accepting that input on the update that detects a stale epoch or loss of health. Bridge control must use the valid left feedback and beam load while right feedback is ineligible.

Install a ROS executable `recover_pair`, runnable as `ros2 run coupled_handover recover_pair --ros-args -r __ns:=/LIFT`. At startup the caller has loaded and configured both upper applications and activated `pair` and `tracking`. Discover actual native manager claims and monitor the relative topic `pair_authority` using `std_msgs/msg/String` with JSON fields `reported_epoch`, `required_epoch`, `healthy` (boolean), `sample` (nonnegative sequence), and `stamp_ns` (the local Linux monotonic clock). The publisher sends fresh authority records around 50 Hz. Ignore malformed, future-dated, and over-100-ms-old authority samples. A higher positive required epoch is an authority floor even on a healthy-false record. Lower authority floors cannot restore tracking.

On ineligible right feedback, strictly switch the native upper owner to `bridge` within 300 ms. Keep `pair` active with both hardware effort interfaces. During a restart there may be several current-stamped, healthy-looking observations from a lower reported epoch. These must not activate tracking. Restore `tracking` within 350 ms after three increasing current-epoch samples have arrived with health true and fresh times. Reset that consecutive evidence on invalid health or generation. Confirm switches through native `list_controllers` and leave the lower controller out of every switch. Native `switch_controller` calls use `STRICT` and exchange both upper controllers as a group.

## Lift performance

Implement a closed loop over the measured beam height and skew, with individual force commands even when only the left end has position feedback. The reference changes during the restart and the load on one side can change. Remain on the requested trajectory through the switch rather than holding at the switch height. Hold and resume is reserved for a loss of observable beam state, which is independent of the right endpoint restart described here.

The manager updates at 100 Hz. Each force is in the inclusive range 0 to 105 N. Respect a force slew rate of 100 N/s per drive. The actuator also limits applied force. The commanded peak changes are measured independently across update periods with allowance for scheduling jitter. Keep peak absolute skew within 0.035 m and peak absolute internal force within 4.5 N. During an observable outage, cover at least 75% of the height increase requested over that same interval. Across the movement, keep RMS height error at most 0.022 m and final height error at most 0.012 m. The independent plant uses unequal loads on its two drives, viscous drag, and signed elastic coupling. The called fixture varies load distribution, coupling parameters, and when feedback restarts. The native claims and applied actuator forces determine the result.

Keep realtime plugin callbacks allocation- and filesystem-independent after initialization. Submit the sources and build rules. The baseline Jazzy examples and the interface map are in `/app/examples/README.md`.
